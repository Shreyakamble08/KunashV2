Core Capabilities

Fast Product Search

Quickly find and select products during order creation with instant search and category browsing.

Smart Order Management

Build and review orders from a dedicated workspace with clear totals and item control.

Customer Details

Capture customer information when needed without slowing down the checkout flow.

Table Number Support

Associate orders with table information for service and dine-in operations.

Flexible Discounts

Apply discounts directly during checkout with immediate total updates.
