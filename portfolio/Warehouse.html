Inside the system

Inventory & Warehouse Management

Complete visibility across every warehouse.

Centralize inventory, warehouse operations, and stock movement with real-time visibility and complete operational accuracy.

  • ✓ Real-time inventory tracking with live stock availability across warehouses, racks, and bin locations.
  • ✓ Multi-warehouse & bin management with configurable put-away strategies and internal stock transfers.
  • ✓ Batch, lot & expiry tracking for complete product traceability, quality control, and compliance.
  • ✓ Automated stock adjustments through purchase receipts, sales dispatches, returns, and physical stock audits.
  • ✓ Inventory insights & alerts including low-stock notifications, stock ageing, movement history, and warehouse performance reports.

Sales & Dispatch

From order confirmed to doorstep, in view.

A sales order becomes a dispatch plan, a delivery challan, and an invoice each stage visible and traceable, with returns handled the same way.

  • ✓ Dispatch planning that groups orders by route and bay.
  • ✓ Shipment tracking from dock to delivery.
  • ✓ Returns management linked back to the original order.
Finance & Billing

Know what you earned, not just what you shipped.

Receivables, payables, and expenses roll up automatically into a profitability view with GST handled as part of the same flow, not a separate spreadsheet.

  • ✓ Receivables & payables aged and tracked to source order.
  • ✓ GST management built into every invoice and return.
  • ✓ Profitability by order, product, or customer.

How data moves

One pipeline, four stages, zero re-entry.

Every module feeds the next nothing is typed twice.

01

Procure

Purchase orders raised against suppliers in Master Management, tied to agreed units and pricing.

02

Store

Goods received into Inventory and allocated to bins through Warehouse Management.

03

Fulfil

Sales orders become dispatch plans, delivery challans, and invoices in Sales & Dispatch.

04

Reconcile

Payments, GST, and margins settle in Finance & Billing, visible in Reports & Analytics.

Engineering approach

Built to stay maintainable, not just to launch.

Modular by design

Each module owns its own data and screens, so new modules can be added without reworking the rest.

Role-based access everywhere

Every action, not just every page, checks permissions before it runs.

Real-time by default

Stock, orders, and cash figures update the moment an action is taken — no nightly batch jobs.

Audit trail on every action

Who changed what, and when, is recorded automatically across every module.
